# Data licence and changes

The files in `data/` are derived from **Inside Airbnb** data.

> Data from Inside Airbnb (insideairbnb.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0
> (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified: see below.

Inside Airbnb's download page (read on 2026-11-19) says: *"Our publicly downloadable
datasets are currently made available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0) licence"* and asks users to *"credit Inside Airbnb as the source."*

**Source:** Prague, scrape of 2026-09-20 (`reviews.csv.gz`, `listings.csv.gz`). The exact
URLs and download date are in `data/manifest.json` under `source`.

## What was changed

Every change is listed per review in `data/manifest.json`.

| Change | Details |
|---|---|
| Sampled | One listing; 300–500 of its reviews, keeping the full date range and language mix. Automated "host cancelled" notes and comments under 20 characters were dropped. |
| Fields removed | `reviewer_id`, `reviewer_name`. Reviews get new ids (`r0001`…); `idMap` links them to Inside Airbnb review ids. |
| Names redacted | Person names inside review text are replaced with `[name]`. Positions are in `redactions`. |
| Dates changed | Some real cleanliness complaints were moved into one month to plant a known trend. Original and new dates are in `redate.moves`. |
| Ratings added | Inside Airbnb has no per-review ratings. Each review's ratings were generated by an LLM from its text (model and prompt in `ratings`); fields the owner corrected are marked `"owner"` in `ratings_source`. |
| Labels added | `data/labels/` holds topic labels and rating checks made by hand. |

Review text is otherwise unchanged, in its original language.

## Privacy

Names and reviewer IDs are removed. The original reviews remain public on Airbnb and in
Inside Airbnb's dataset, so published reviews can be matched to them; this dataset is not
anonymised.
